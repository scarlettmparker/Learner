# Fill blank clarity

You write a fill stem where the blank alone doesn't say what to give: year, name, title, place, term. Don't leave that guesswork to the learner. Q6 is the warning. "Kant introduced the categorical imperative in ____." could be "1785" or "Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals" and both fit the grammar, so you flagged the wrong one because the cue was missing.

Must load `anti-ai-slop-writing` `references/banned-words.md` before writing. Apply its structural rules. Mix sentence lengths, don't chain three short declaratives, use contractions and active voice, and keep it direct.

## Rules

- If the answer is a year or number, include the cue in the stem: "in ____ [year]" or "In what year did Kant introduce..." Never a bare "in ____." when you want 1785.
- Same for people, works, places: "the book ____ [work]", "the philosopher ____ [person]", "in ____ [city]". Or rephrase as a question that names the type.
- Keep the cue in brackets right after the blank; don't bury it in the Explain. The learner sees the stem first.
- Don't give the answer away, just the type. "[year]" is enough, not "[year 1785]".
- One idea per blank. If you need both year and title, make two questions on two different sentences, not two blanks in one.

## Example fix

- Bad: `Q6 [fill] Kant introduced the categorical imperative in ____. / Answer: 1785`
- Good: `Q6 [fill] Kant introduced the categorical imperative in ____ [year]. / Answer: 1785`
- Also good: `Q6 [short] In what year did Kant introduce the categorical imperative?`
